# ACME Robotics — Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

## 1. Working Hours and Flexibility

Standard working hours at ACME Robotics are 09:30 to 17:30 in the employee's
local timezone. Core collaboration hours, during which all staff are expected to
be reachable, run from 11:00 to 15:00 UTC. Outside of core hours, employees are
free to arrange their schedule as they see fit, provided their commitments to
their team are met.

Employees may request a compressed four-day week after completing twelve months
of continuous service. Compressed-week requests are approved by the employee's
direct manager and reviewed by People Operations every six months.

## 2. Remote and Hybrid Work

ACME Robotics operates a hybrid-first model. Employees assigned to an office
location are expected on site a minimum of eight days per calendar month.
Fully remote contracts are available for roles designated "location
independent" in the job requisition.

Employees working remotely from a country other than their contracted country
of employment must notify People Operations in advance. Stays exceeding
thirty consecutive days require prior written approval, because they may
create a permanent establishment tax liability for the company.

## 3. Paid Time Off

All full-time employees accrue twenty-five days of paid annual leave per year,
in addition to public holidays observed in their country of employment. Leave
accrues monthly at a rate of 2.08 days per completed month of service.

Employees may carry over a maximum of five unused annual leave days into the
following calendar year. Carried-over days must be used before 31 March, after
which they expire without compensation. ACME Robotics does not pay out unused
annual leave except where required by local statute upon termination.

Sick leave is uncapped and does not require a medical certificate for absences
of four consecutive working days or fewer. Absences of five consecutive working
days or more require a certificate from a registered medical practitioner.

## 4. Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to twenty-six weeks of parental leave at full
pay. Secondary caregivers are entitled to twelve weeks at full pay. Both
entitlements are available to birth, adoptive, and surrogate parents without
distinction, and may be taken in up to three separate blocks within the first
twenty-four months following birth or placement.

Employees returning from parental leave are entitled to a phased return: for
the first eight weeks back, they may work at eighty percent of contracted hours
at full pay.

## 5. Expenses and Equipment

Employees may expense reasonable business costs without pre-approval up to a
limit of 400 EUR per individual item. Items above this threshold require
written approval from a budget holder before purchase. Expense claims must be
submitted within sixty days of the date the cost was incurred; claims submitted
later than this are rejected automatically by the finance system.

Each employee receives a home-office equipment budget of 1,200 EUR, refreshed
every three years. Laptops are replaced on a four-year cycle, or earlier if a
hardware fault cannot be economically repaired. Equipment purchased under the
home-office budget remains the property of ACME Robotics and must be returned
upon termination of employment.

Travel booked for business purposes must use the company travel platform.
Economy class is the default for all flights under six hours of scheduled
flight time. Flights of six hours or more may be booked in premium economy.
Business class requires VP-level approval regardless of duration.

## 6. Performance and Compensation

Performance reviews are conducted twice per year, in April and October.
Compensation adjustments arising from the April review take effect on 1 July;
adjustments from the October review take effect on 1 January.

The performance rating scale has five levels: Below Expectations, Developing,
Meeting Expectations, Exceeding Expectations, and Outstanding. A rating of
Below Expectations initiates a structured sixty-day performance improvement
plan, jointly owned by the employee, their manager, and a People Operations
partner.

## 7. Learning and Development

Every employee has an annual professional development allowance of 2,000 EUR,
which may be spent on conferences, courses, certifications, or books. The
allowance does not carry over between years. Requests are approved by the
employee's manager and do not require finance sign-off if within the allowance.

Employees who complete an approved certification relevant to their role receive
a one-time bonus of 500 EUR. A maximum of two certification bonuses may be
claimed per calendar year.
